## 2026-11-02 — Word Search squares are at least 60pt; the phone grid is capped by width (#560)

**Decision (owner, through the coordinator on PR #563; built by Claude):** every Word Search letter
square is 60 to 64pt. The level table (iPhone 6, 7, 8, 8, 8; iPad 6, 8, 9, 10, 10 by level band) is
what a level wants; the grid dealt is the largest that fits that wanted size with 60pt squares across the
measured width, never under 5×5, the way Sudoku deals by width. So an iPhone SE plays 6×6, one with
Display Zoom (320pt) 5×5, a 393pt iPhone 6×6 and a 440pt one up to 7×7, while a portrait iPad keeps its
table at 64pt. The word count is capped at the grid's side. A board that no longer fits after a width
change is dealt again; one that still fits keeps its progress.

**Why:** AGENTS.md and conventions §4 set touch targets at 60pt or more. The issue's 44pt was a
mistake, and at 320pt an 8×8 grid gave 38pt squares. More words than the side often took hundreds of
re-rolls in a test run of the generator.

**Revisit when:** a playtest shows the smaller phone grids are too easy at levels 7–10.

Ludavela

Warm, simple games for an active mind. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Why I made Ludavela

I made Ludavela mainly for my dad. He was diagnosed with the early stages of dementia, and since then I have looked for activities he enjoys that keep his mind active.

I also made it for myself, my sister and my nephews and nieces, because this illness runs in our family. I wanted something that invites us to exercise our minds through play, and I want to share it with everyone.

I hope that what I can build, with the help of AI, will keep not only my dad company, but as many people as possible.

Por qué hice Ludavela

Hice Ludavela, sobre todo, para mi papá. A él le diagnosticaron los inicios de una demencia, y desde entonces busco actividades que disfrute y que mantengan su mente activa.

También la hice para mí, para mi hermana y para mis sobrinos, porque esta enfermedad está presente en nuestra familia. Quería algo que nos invitara a ejercitar la mente jugando, y quiero compartirlo con todas las personas.

Espero que lo que sé hacer, con ayuda de la IA, sirva para acompañar no solo a mi papá, sino a la mayor cantidad de personas posible.
